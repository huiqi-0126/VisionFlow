# Seven-shot blueprint

Apply the user's style lock to every prompt. Keep the same home identity across all seven shots.

1. **Kitchen wide** — View from dining area into the kitchen; large window; one resident small in frame at the back counter.
2. **Breakfast tray** — Top-down hand-held tray near a window; neatly arranged breakfast with eggs, avocado, greens, berries, red vegetables, and a protein or culturally appropriate substitute.
3. **Narrow counter** — Entrance view along a long cooking/coffee counter; window, pendant, towel, sink, and a few restrained objects; no person.
4. **Stair movement** — Double-height living space with sculptural stairs; one resident moving calmly; ceiling fixture or fan visible.
5. **Laptop POV** — Hands using a laptop at a dining or coffee table; the home's window and seating remain visible; no readable screen text.
6. **Living-room overhead** — High-angle view from an upper level; major window, sofa, rug, and coffee table establish the home geometry.
7. **Exterior blue hour** — Front elevation at dusk; warm interior/practical lighting contrasts with the cool sky and landscaping.

## Shared negative constraints

No generic showroom substitution, unrelated architecture, extra rooms, new residents, distorted hands, duplicated furniture, changing window geometry, illegible captions, logos, watermarks, or readable UI.
